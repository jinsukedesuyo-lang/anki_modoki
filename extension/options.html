The URL where you published the app on GitHub Pages (for local testing: http://localhost:8080/)

How to use

  1. Turn on Language Reactor subtitles on YouTube or Netflix
  2. Select the unknown word with your mouse (double-click works too)
  3. Press Alt+A (or click the toolbar icon / right-click → "Make an Anki Modoki card")
  4. The video pauses and the Add screen opens with the subtitle line, the highlighted word and a screenshot
  5. Add the meaning and the core image, then save

You can change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts. On Netflix, screenshots may come out black because of copy protection.
